AQS Privacy Policy

Privacy information for the ArchTek Nexus Quant System (AQS), including account services, market information, notifications, subscriptions, and related application services.

Current Policy AQS — ArchTek Nexus Quant System
Our approach to privacy

ArchTek Nexus is committed to handling information responsibly and transparently. AQS is designed to process the information required to provide its account, subscription, notification, and market-information features.

Account Information

AQS uses account authentication services to provide user account functionality. Information associated with an AQS account may be processed for authentication, account management, and providing the application's supported features.

Account Deletion

AQS provides users with a direct account deletion option within the application through the Account & Privacy section.

Users who no longer have access to the application can also request deletion of their AQS account and associated data through our external account deletion page.

Request Account Deletion →

Market Data

AQS retrieves public market data from external sources and processes that information to display market conditions, market statistics, and regime classifications.

Market information is provided for informational and analytical purposes. AQS does not execute financial trades or transactions on behalf of users.

Notifications

If enabled by the user, AQS may send notifications relating to market alerts, including price thresholds, structural changes, and other supported market conditions.

Subscriptions & Payments

AQS may provide subscription-based features. Purchases and subscription transactions distributed through Google Play are processed using the applicable Google Play billing services.

AQS does not directly process or store the user's payment card details.

Third-Party Services

AQS may use third-party services required to provide application functionality, authentication, notifications, subscriptions, distribution, and infrastructure services.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

Data Usage

Information processed by AQS is used to provide and maintain application functionality, authenticate accounts, support subscriptions, deliver enabled notifications, and provide market information and regime analysis.

AQS does not use the application to execute financial trades or transactions.

Data Security

ArchTek Nexus takes reasonable measures to protect information processed by AQS. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security, and users should understand that information transmitted over the internet may carry inherent security risks.

Privacy Requests & Contact

For privacy-related questions, data requests, or assistance with account deletion, contact the ArchTek Nexus support team:

support@archteknexus.co.za

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when AQS functionality, services, or applicable requirements change. The current version will remain available at this web address.
