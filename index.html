AQS Privacy Policy

AQS (ArchTek Nexus Quant System) respects user privacy and is designed to collect and process only the information required to provide its account, subscription, notification, and market-information features.

Account Information

AQS uses account authentication services to provide user account functionality. Information associated with an AQS account may be processed for authentication, account management, and providing the application's features.

Account Deletion

Users may request deletion of their AQS account from within the application through the Account & Privacy section.

A web-based account deletion request is also available at: https://archteknexus.com/delete-account

When an account deletion request is submitted, AQS processes the request according to the account and data associated with that service.

Market Data

AQS retrieves public market data from external sources and processes that information to display market conditions, market statistics, and regime classifications.

Market data is not used to execute trades or financial transactions on behalf of the user.

Notifications

If enabled by the user, AQS may send notifications relating to market alerts, including price thresholds, structural changes, and other supported market conditions.

Subscriptions and Payments

AQS may provide subscription-based features. Purchases and subscription transactions distributed through Google Play are processed using the applicable Google Play billing services.

AQS does not directly process or store the user's payment card details.

Third-Party Services

AQS may use third-party services required to provide application functionality, authentication, notifications, subscriptions, distribution, and infrastructure services.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

Data Usage

Information processed by AQS is used to provide and maintain the application's functionality, authenticate accounts, support subscriptions, deliver enabled notifications, and provide market information and regime analysis.

Market information displayed by AQS is provided for informational and analytical purposes only. AQS does not execute financial trades or transactions within the application.

Data Security

ArchTek Nexus takes reasonable measures to protect information processed by AQS. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security, and users should understand that information transmitted over the internet may carry inherent security risks.

Privacy Requests and Contact

For privacy-related questions, data requests, or assistance with account deletion, contact:

support@archteknexus.co.za

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when AQS functionality, services, or applicable requirements change. The current version of this policy will be made available at this web address.
