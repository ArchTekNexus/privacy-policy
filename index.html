AQS Privacy Policy

AQS (ArchTek Nexus Quant System) respects user privacy.

Data Collection

AQS does not collect personal user data. The application retrieves public market data from external sources to display market conditions and regime classifications.

Notifications

If enabled by the user, AQS may send notifications related to market alerts such as price thresholds or structural regime changes.

Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party services including Google Play services for distribution, billing functionality, and system stability.

Data Usage

Market data displayed in the application is used solely for informational purposes. No financial transactions or trading activity occur inside the application.
